The woff2 files in this folder (Fira Code, Fira Sans, IBM Plex Mono, VT323) are Latin
subsets downloaded from Google Fonts. Each is released under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org); the copyright holders are listed on each
font's Google Fonts page.

daedra.woff is a separate font with its own terms; see the comment above its @font-face
rule in css/style.css.
